1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
Solize (Solar Plant Analyzer) is a comprehensive, standalone desktop software 
application designed for solar power plant operators, performance engineers, 
and renewable energy asset managers. The platform bridges the gap between raw, 
complex industrial operational logs and high-level performance intelligence.

By combining an intuitive dark-themed user interface (React & TypeScript) with 
a high-performance backend analytics engine (FastAPI & Pandas), Solize automates 
data ingestion, key performance indicator (KPI) calculations, anomaly detection, 
and automated report generation in PDF and Microsoft Word formats—all within a 
secure, offline-first desktop environment.


2. SYSTEM PURPOSE & THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES
------------------------------------------
Problem Context:
Modern utility-scale and commercial solar power plants generate vast amounts 
of data daily across inverters, weather stations, and meters. Typically, this 
operational data is exported as multi-sheet, unstandardized Excel workbooks 
containing thousands of rows, missing entries, and data anomalies. Manually 
compiling, cleaning, and calculating metrics from these logs is extremely 
time-consuming, prone to human error, and delays operational decision-making.

System Purpose:
Solize was built to completely automate and streamline this workflow. Its 
primary objectives are:
- Instant Data Ingestion: Parse and clean multi-sheet operational Excel files 
  in seconds regardless of row count or missing data fields.
- Automated KPI Calculation: Compute standard solar metrics (Performance Ratio, 
  Specific Yield, Capacity Utilization Factor, Degradation Rates) with mathematical precision.
- Actionable Diagnostic Insights: Pinpoint underperforming inverters, 
  excessive soiling/shading losses, and equipment degradation trends.
- Automated Reporting: Eliminate manual report creation by generating formatted 
  executive PDF and Word reports with a single click.
- Offline Data Security: Ensure sensitive plant data stays 100% local on the 
  user's machine without relying on external cloud connections or subscriptions.


3. RELEVANCE TO SOLAR POWER PLANT OPERATIONS
--------------------------------------------
Solize addresses core operational and engineering challenges in solar asset management:

A. Performance Ratio (PR %) Calculation & Tracking:
   PR compares actual plant energy generation against theoretical output under 
   real irradiance and temperature conditions. Solize computes daily, monthly, 
   and annual PR to verify if the plant meets guaranteed performance levels.

B. Inverter-Level Health & Conversion Monitoring:
   Solar inverters convert DC power from PV strings to grid-ready AC power. 
   Solize parses individual inverter data to detect conversion efficiency drops, 
   thermal derating, and clipping losses early, preventing costly downtime.

C. Solar PV Panel Degradation Modeling:
   Solar modules degrade naturally by 0.5% to 0.8% per year. Solize tracks 
   historical multi-year trend lines to calculate real annual degradation rates, 
   helping asset managers enforce manufacturer warranty claims.

D. Energy Loss Breakdown & Diagnostics:
   Solize categorizes losses (soiling, temperature losses, inverter outages, 
   grid curtailment), enabling Operations & Maintenance (O&M) teams to schedule 
   targeted panel cleaning or equipment maintenance.

E. Financial & Capacity Utilization Analysis:
   By computing Specific Yield (kWh/kWp) and Capacity Utilization Factor (CUF %), 
   Solize gives investors and asset owners clear visibility into financial returns 
   and plant utilization.


4. MATHEMATICAL EQUATIONS & FORMULAS USED IN SOLIZE
---------------------------------------------------

A. Performance Ratio (PR %)
   The primary metric measuring overall solar plant efficiency:

   PR (%) = [ Actual Generation (kWh) / Theoretical Max Generation (kWh) ] × 100%

   Detailed Formula implemented in Solize:
   PR (%) = [ Generation (kWh) / ( Σ Irradiance (W/m²) × Panel Area (m²) × Module Efficiency × Running Hours / 1000 ) ] × 100%

   Where:
   - Σ Irradiance = Sum of daily solar irradiance values (W/m² or Wh/m²)
   - Panel Area = Total surface area of installed PV modules (m²)
   - Module Efficiency = PV panel efficiency expressed as a fraction (e.g., 0.21 for 21%)
   - Running Hours = Assumed daily peak sun hours / operating hours (e.g., 5.5 hrs/day)
   - Division by 1000 converts Wh to kWh.


B. Capacity Utilization Factor (CUF %)
   Measures real energy generation output against continuous full-capacity output over a time period:

   CUF (%) = [ Total Generation (MWh) / ( Installed AC Capacity (MW) × Total Hours ) ] × 100%

   Where:
   - Total Hours = Total Days in period × 24 hours/day


C. Specific Yield (kWh/kWp)
   Measures energy produced per installed DC peak kilowatt capacity:

   Specific Yield = Total Generation (kWh) / Installed DC Capacity (kWp)

   Where:
   - Installed DC Capacity (kWp) = Installed DC Capacity (MW) × 1000


D. Plant Availability (%)
   Measures the operational uptime ratio of the plant:

   Availability (%) = [ Actual Generation (kWh) / Total Possible Generation (kWh) ] × 100%


E. Inverter-Level Performance Ratio (PR_inv %)
   Evaluates individual inverter efficiency across assigned sub-array panel areas:

   PR_inv (%) = [ Inverter Generation (kWh) / ( Σ Irradiance × Inverter Panel Area × Module Efficiency × Running Hours / 1000 ) ] × 100%

   Where:
   - Inverter Panel Area (m²) = Total Plant Panel Area (m²) / Total Number of Inverters


F. Annual Degradation Rate (% / year)
   Calculates observed module performance degradation over time using Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Linear Regression on monthly PR trends:

   Slope (m) = Σ [ (x_i - x_mean) × (y_i - y_mean) ] / Σ [ (x_i - x_mean)² ]

   Where:
   - x_i = Month index (0, 1, 2, ..., N-1)
   - y_i = Monthly Performance Ratio (%)
   - Monthly Decline Rate = Slope (m)
   - Annual Degradation Rate (%/year) = | Slope (m) × 12 |


G. Expected Cumulative Degradation (%)
   Predicts baseline degradation over elapsed plant lifetime:

   Expected Degradation (%) = Plant Age (Years) × Configured Annual Degradation Rate (%/year)

   Where:
   - Plant Age (Years) = (Study End Date - Commission Date in days) / 365.25


5. KEY SYSTEM MODULES & FEATURES
---------------------------------
- Solar Plant Profile Management: Create, edit, and manage multiple solar plant 
  profiles with custom technical specifications (DC/AC capacity, inverter counts, 
  tilt angles, module efficiency).
- Robust Excel Ingestion Engine: Drag-and-drop import of daily/monthly inverter 
  sheets. Auto-filters NaN values, missing dates, and formatting discrepancies.
- Interactive Analytics Dashboard: Rich dark-mode dashboard displaying generation 
  timelines, PR trends, inverter rank comparisons, and weather correlation charts.
- Alert & Anomaly Engine: Automatic detection of underperforming inverters, 
  PR drops below thresholds, and data gaps.
- Executive Report Generator: Instant export of customizable performance reports 
  (PDF & DOCX) featuring plant summary tables, loss breakdown charts, and O&M 
  recommendations.


6. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & DEPLOYMENT
--------------------------------------
Solize utilizes a hybrid web-to-desktop architecture:

- Frontend Layer: React (Vite) + TypeScript + Recharts + Vanilla CSS Dark Theme.
- Backend Layer: Python 3.9 + FastAPI (Asynchronous REST API) + SQLAlchemy 2.0 ORM.
- Analytics & Computing: Pandas + NumPy + Matplotlib.
- Document Compilation: ReportLab (PDF) + python-docx (Microsoft Word).
- Database: Embedded SQLite stored locally under %APPDATA%\Solizer\data\solizer.db.
- Desktop GUI Wrapper: PyWebView (Edge Chromium / WebView2 native OS window 
  wrapper at 1280x720 resolution).
- Single Executable Packaging: PyInstaller (bundles backend, frontend dist, 
  and runtime into solizer-backend.exe).
- Distribution: Inno Setup compiler generating a standalone Windows Installer 
  (Solizer_Setup.exe).
